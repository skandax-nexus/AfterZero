> the Keepers of the Hour (Mother Ondine Halden), the Rememberers, costed endings and the harder level climaxes.

Target length: **~2 hours** (Prologue 6 min · 8 levels × 12–15 min · 3 interludes · Epilogue 5 min).

## Premise
Veyra is a city built around a clock tower that does more than tell time. Its Founders built the **Zero Engine** in
the tower's core to hold three versions of the city together: **PAST**, **PRESENT** and **FUTURE**. Every night at
the second ring of the bell the city collapses, and every morning it begins again. Nobody remembers it happening,
except the things that break and stay broken.

**Arin Vale** arrives on the last train with no memory, a coat that doesn't fit the season, and a letter addressed
to Arin, dated tomorrow, sealed with the clock-tower seal: a ring with a zero in the middle, **(0)**.

## The truth (revealed gradually)
1. Forty years ago the three states began to grind against each other. The Founders calculated a **larger collapse**:
   all three states would fall into one, erasing everyone in every era.
2. The Founders chose to **evacuate quietly**. They drew up an evacuation list nine days before the official
   catastrophe date (L4) and removed the evacuated families from the public record (L5) so the panic would not spread.
3. The children were hidden in a pocket that exists between the states: **the Nursery With No Address** (L6).
   The catastrophe was the Founders' attempted rescue going wrong, not an accident and not an attack.
4. **Ilse Marrow**, the clockkeeper, saw the rescue failing and **stopped the tower from inside** (L2) to buy time.
   The official story was an earthquake.
5. The second ring of the bell is triggered by **a signal from the tower's future ruins** (L7). Someone in the Future
   keeps restarting the night.
6. That someone is **Arin, from another branch** of time. In a branch where the larger collapse happened, Arin
   survived alone, found the Zero Engine, and **restarted the cycle**, trading one repeating night for the
   survival of everyone. The intervention is signed in Arin's own handwriting (L8). The Arin we play is the
   "next" Arin: the cycle's own fail-safe, sent in to check whether the city is ready to stop repeating.
7. **Maren Oskel** is the thread. Listed as both alive and dead in the archive (L3), she is the child Arin rescues
   in the Drowned District (L5). As an adult outside time she is **The Witness** (L6–L8), who has been guiding Arin
   with letters all along. The letter dated tomorrow is in her handwriting, copied from Arin's.

## Act structure
**Act I — The Repeat (Prologue, L1, L2).** Arin learns the rule: things persist. The tower was stopped from inside.
**Interlude A — "Pell's Route"**: on the walk to the archive, Pell admits he has delivered the same letter to Arin
"more times than there are days".

**Act II — The Record (L3, L4, L5).** Evidence triangulation: a woman both alive and dead, an evacuation list older
than the disaster, families erased from memory. Arin saves the child (Maren) and must choose records or keepsake.
**Interlude B — "The Lift Down"**: Ignatz, in an echo, says his sister Ilse "always carried a carved zero".

**Act III — The Seam (L6, L7, L8).** The Nursery proves the catastrophe was a rescue. The Witness speaks.
Arin lives through the catastrophe itself, finds the signal in the future ruins, and reaches the Zero Engine.
**Interlude C — "Before the Second Bell"**: The Witness: "You've done this before. You chose differently."

## Endings (choice in L8, flavoured by the L5 choice)
- **Stability**: Arin keeps the cycle running but softens it: the city repeats, but its people now remember a little
  each time. Epilogue: the festival again; Pell hands Arin the letter and says "welcome back" and means it.
- **Evidence**: Arin preserves the record and lets the cycle crack. The Founders' secret becomes public; the states
  begin to separate. Epilogue: the horizon opens onto regions with different physical laws, the setting of Game 02.
- L5 choice variant: if Arin saved the **records**, Odile publishes the names in the Epilogue. If Arin saved the
  **keepsake**, Maren keeps her music box, and the lullaby plays over the final shot.

## Recurring motifs
- The **zero (0)**: on the seal, the carved toy in the Nursery, the socket in the Zero Chamber.
- **The bell rings twice**: the first ring is a warning, the second is the collapse.
- **The blue cup**: the first thing Arin saves. It reappears intact on the Zero Chamber's altar.
- **The lullaby**: four notes, with one missing in each state; complete only in the Nursery.

## Optional content (adds ~15 min)
**Memory Fragments** (3 per level, 24 total) are hologram shards hidden off the main path. Each adds a journal page
from a citizen's life in one of the states. Collecting all 24 unlocks an extra Epilogue line from Ilse.
