# AFTERZERO — The First Cycle — Design v2 ("Hard Cut")

Goals: a **stronger plot** (personal stakes, a real antagonist, two tempting endings), **harder levels** (every level
ends in a demanding climax that combines its mechanics), and a **large recurring cast** (26 named characters).
Length target: ~2 h on Normal, ~2.5 h on Hard.

---

## 1. The story, rebuilt

### The personal stake: who Arin really is
Arin Vale was **one of the fourteen children** the Founders hid in the Nursery forty years ago. Clockkeeper
**Ilse Marrow** was Arin's guardian. In one branch of time the rescue failed and the three states collapsed; Arin
was the only child pulled out alive, grew up in the wreckage, found the Zero Engine and **restarted the night**,
then sent a younger, memory-wiped self back in as the cycle's fail-safe. That younger self is the Arin we play.

Arin carries one object from the start: a **brass pocket watch engraved "For A. — keep time for me. I.M."** It
doesn't tick. It starts ticking in Level 08.

Arin hums a lullaby without knowing where it comes from. It's the Nursery lullaby; Arin's version is the only one
with all four notes.

### The antagonists
- **Founder Aurel Halden** ordered the secret evacuation and the erasure of the families. He believes silence saved
  more lives than truth would have. He appears as recordings and echoes, and is physically present on catastrophe
  night (L7). He is not a villain: he is the strongest argument for Stability.
- **The Keepers of the Hour** are a Present-day order who sense the repeat and want it to continue forever, because
  inside the cycle nobody ever dies for good. They are led by **Mother Ondine Halden**, Aurel's daughter. Keepers
  hunt "anomalies" like Arin: they lock doors, burn records, patrol with sight-lanterns and run the vibration
  machine.
- **The Rememberers** are a small underground of people who keep tallies of the loops. They are led by archivist
  **Odile Fenwick** and courier **Pell Ashgrove**, and they want the truth out, whatever it costs.

So the final choice is a real argument between people you have met. Stability (Ondine and Halden) keeps everyone
alive in a remembering loop. Evidence (Odile and Pell) frees time, but some people stuck in the loop will not
survive the separation.

### Reveal schedule (one big reveal per act, smaller ones per level)
| When | Reveal |
|---|---|
| L01 | The letter is in Arin's own handwriting, aged and shaky |
| L02 | Ilse stopped the tower from inside. Her logbook mentions "my little A." |
| L03 | Maren Oskel is both dead and transferred. Arin countersigned the record |
| L04 | The evacuation list includes **"VALE, A., age 8. Guardian: I. Marrow"** (end of Act II hook) |
| L05 | Maren recognises Arin: "You're the one who hums" |
| L06 | **The Witness is Maren**. Arin's name is carved inside cot 7 (moved from L08 so L08 isn't overloaded) |
| L07 | The signal from the future ruins is Arin's handwriting. Halden admits he knew the rescue might fail |
| L08 | Arin from the other branch: "I restarted the night. You are the version of me that gets to choose." The watch starts ticking |

### Endings
- **Stability** (Ondine, Halden): the loop continues, but everyone now remembers a little more each night. Arin
  stays as the city's keeper of time, like Ilse. *Cost:* Arin never leaves; Maren stays in the seam.
  *Hook:* the Keepers now answer to Arin.
- **Evidence** (Odile, Pell): the names are published and the states separate. *Cost:* the loop-born (people who
  only exist because of the repeat, such as Pell's daughter Lise) fade at dawn. *Hook:* the horizon opens onto
  lands with different physical laws (Game 02).
- The L05 choice (register or music box) and the L07 outcome (how many groups survived) change Epilogue scenes.
- Collecting all 24 memory fragments adds a final scene: an echo of Ilse winds the watch.

## 3. Difficulty
A new **difficulty setting** (Story / Normal / Hard), chosen on New Game and changeable in Pause, scales:

| | Story | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objective hints | after 45 s | after 120 s | never |
| Crisis timers | ×1.5 | ×1.0 | ×0.75 |
| Machine / Keeper hearing and sight | ×0.6 | ×1.0 | ×1.35 |
| Checkpoints | all | all | climax checkpoints removed |
| Wrong answers in deduction puzzles | free | 10 s time penalty or alarm | reset the puzzle |
| Decoys (L04) | unlimited | 3 | 2 |

## 4. Level-by-level: hard design

Every level follows: **learn** (safe observation area) → **combine** (two mechanics together) → **climax** (a
demanding final challenge that combines all of the level's mechanics under pressure, with a checkpoint before it).

### PROLOGUE — The Last Train (6 min)
Teaches walking, reading, talking. Sefa: "One more off than on." Arin finds the stopped watch in a coat pocket. The
Witness speaks once over the train's intercom: "Don't be afraid of the bell."

### L01 — The Bell That Rings Twice (14 min)
- **New:** the 30-second loop, Past shifting, **Sister Vossel** watching for anomalies.
- **Hard twist:** Vossel patrols the street. If she sees Arin shift state or tamper with an object 3 times in one
  loop, the Keepers **shorten the loop** (30 → 24 → 18 s).
- **Climax, the "Perfect Loop":** to save the cup for good, three things must happen in one loop:
  1. The cart is chocked in the Past.
  2. Lise Ashgrove is stopped from running into the stall at 0:14.
  3. Pell's route is diverted so he doesn't block the escape lane.
- The player has to plan the order and route.
- **Reveal:** the tower letter's handwriting matches the stopped watch's engraving style. Arin's own hand.

### L02 — Under the Clock Skin (15 min)
- **New:** three-state vertical traversal and **moving gear platforms** (the player rides rotating teeth in the
  Past).
- **Combine:** draining the Present chamber starts a slow **refill timer**. The player must climb before the water
  returns, using Past gears as stairs at moments when the Present has no floor.
- **Climax:** **Brother Calder** cuts power and locks the bell floor. The player must restart the Past clockwork,
  shift to the Future to cross the beam in timed wind gusts (telegraphed by banners and a howl), then shift back to
  unlock Calder's door from the other side. Calder escapes: a recurring threat.
- **Reveal:** Ilse's recording: "There was no earthquake. I stopped it. Keep time for me, my little A."

### L03 — The Rain Archive (15 min)
- **New:** evidence triangulation and water routing.
- **Pressure:** **the Keepers are burning the records in the Present.** Fire spreads along the shelves (telegraphed
  by smoke and crackle). The player must route water channels to put out burning aisles before the evidence is
  lost.
- **Hard deduction:** three clue cards, no direct answer. The player combines clues on the deduction board to pick
  Maren's record out of five near-identical ones. On Normal a wrong pick costs time; on Hard it resets the board.
- **Characters:** Sister Vossel returns and Mother Ondine arrives at the end.
- **Climax:** Ondine corners Arin at the map basin and offers a deal: hand over the record and the loop will "keep
  you safe forever." Refusing triggers a sprint through the flooding stacks to the hatch.
- **Reveal:** the record's countersignature is Arin's.

### L04 — The Street Beneath the Street (16 min, the hardest stealth)
- **New:** sound stealth, moving trams, Keeper patrols with **lantern sight-cones** (sight and sound together).
- **Combine:** the machine hears, the Keepers see. Crouching beats the machine; shadows beat the Keepers; the Past
  hides you from both but has trams.
- **Limited decoys:** 3 pipes on Normal (2 on Hard).
- **Climax: the lift chase.** Brother Calder grabs the cable. The player must hold the lift circuit in the Present
  while Calder hunts them, then ride down as he wrecks the shaft (timed debris, telegraphed).
- **Reveal (end of Act II):** the evacuation list: "VALE, A., age 8. Guardian: I. Marrow." Arin was one of the
  children.

### L05 — The Drowned District (16 min)
- **New:** rescue and meaningful trade-off.
- **Combine:** pump (Past) → sluices (Present) → three homes. Each home has a Future echo that shows what happened
  to that family.
- **Harder rescue:** Maren is trapped behind debris that can only be cleared in the Past while she's held in the
  Present. The player alternates states to shore up the beam step by step as the water rises.
- **Confrontation:** Mother Ondine arrives by boat. A **dialogue choice**:
  - Show her the evacuation list: she hesitates and lets you pass.
  - Lie: she sends the Keepers after you, starting a shorter crisis timer.
- **Climax:** with the flood returning, choose the register or the music box. Maren: "You're the one who hums."

### L06 — The Nursery With No Address (16 min, the hardest puzzle)
- **Interlocking puzzle.** Three systems affect each other:
  - **Hanging shapes:** each one only exists in one state.
  - **The lullaby:** each state's music box is missing a different note; only Arin's hum (a new Hum action) has all
    four.
  - **The drifting door:** it drifts whenever you look away, and moving furniture to reach the shapes blocks your
    view of it.
- The player has to work out an order that keeps the door in view while solving the other two.
- **Echo Sense is essential:** some solutions appear only on echoes.
- **Reveal (Act III):** The Witness is Maren, grown. "I stayed so someone would be here when you came back." Arin's
  name is carved in cot 7. Arin takes Ilse's carved zero.

### L07 — The Bell Catastrophe (18 min, the hardest action)
- **New:** an 8-minute multi-state crisis (×0.75 on Hard).
- **Three groups, three different routes:**
  1. Mirela's market group: blocked by fire in the Present, so the route goes through the Past.
  2. Pell and Lise: trapped behind the collapsing arcade; brace the wall with Past scaffolding.
  3. Warden Roe's group: blocked by the Future surge; ground the surge.
- Groups left behind affect the Epilogue.
- **Founder Halden is at the tram stairs**, refusing to let the evacuation go public. To get past him: present the
  list, the record and the register (an evidence check across levels), or find the alternate route, which is
  harder.
- **Climax:** disable the correct bell mechanism by playing the bell sequence from L02 and L06 under falling
  masonry. Ilse Marrow walks into the tower: "Keep time for me."
- **Reveal:** the signal from the future ruins is in Arin's handwriting.

### L08 — The First Zero (18 min, final)
- **Phase 1:** place the zero. The chamber wakes and starts to break apart.
- **Phase 2:** rebuild the bell sequence with bells spread across the three states, while chamber segments collapse
  on a rhythm (telegraphed).
- **Phase 3, the Tribunal:** echoes of Halden and Ondine argue for Stability; Odile, Pell and Maren argue for
  Evidence. The player answers each in a dialogue sequence.
- **Phase 4:** a playable memory. Child Arin walks the Nursery with Ilse, who gives Arin the watch.
- **The other-branch Arin:** "I restarted the night. You are the version of me that gets to choose." The watch
  starts ticking. **Choose Stability or Evidence.**

### EPILOGUE (6 min)
Changes depending on: the L08 choice, the L05 choice, how many L07 groups were saved, and the 24 fragments.

## 5. New systems this design needs
| System | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GameSettings` (difficulty) | Scales hints, timers, hearing/sight, checkpoints, penalties |
| `KeeperPatrol` | Sight-cone guards with lanterns; spotting the player triggers an alarm/chase; combines with `VibrationSentinel` |
| `Pursuer` | Calder and the Keeper chase sequences |
| `DialogueChoice` | Branching conversations (Ondine, Halden, the Tribunal) with flag outcomes |
| `EvidenceCheck` | Cross-level flag check (list + record + register) |
| `SpreadingFire` | Fire spreading along a path, put out by water-routing flags |
| `MovingPlatform` | Carries the player on gears and lifts |
| `LoopEscalation` | Shortens the L01 loop when Vossel spots anomalies |
| `HumAbility` | Arin's four-note hum used in the L06 puzzle |
| `DeductionBoard` | Pick one answer from candidates using clue cards, with a penalty for wrong answers |
